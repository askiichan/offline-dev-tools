## Design Context

### Users
Creators who need to quickly extract a segment from a local video, preview the cut, and save it without uploading their footage or re-encoding it. The wider app is a privacy-first collection of offline developer tools.

### Brand Personality
Approachable, precise, private. The experience should feel reassuring and straightforward while retaining trustworthy technical detail.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing dark toolset shell and muted blue-gray accents. Make the clip workflow more welcoming and visual than a command-line editor, without introducing a competing theme.

### Design Principles
- Keep media local to the browser; explain export limitations honestly.
- Make selecting and previewing a segment obvious before showing advanced controls.
- Preserve the source streams on export rather than promising frame-exact cuts that require re-encoding.
- Make long-running processing, errors, and large-file limits clear.
- Keep controls keyboard-accessible and usable on narrow screens.
